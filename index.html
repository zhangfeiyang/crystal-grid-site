Crystal Grid — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Crystal Grid (the "App") is an offline block puzzle game. The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

No data collection

  • The App contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • The App makes no network requests. All features work fully offline.
  • Your game progress, best score, coins and settings are stored only on your device (local storage) and are removed when you uninstall the App.

Children

The App contains no chat, social features, or user-generated content sharing, and is safe for all ages.
